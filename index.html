About Me

I'm Nafisa Tabassum

A Computer Science student driven by logic and creativity.

My journey into technology started with curiosity. Growing up as a South Asian woman interested in STEM, I was always drawn to science fairs, small experiments, creative projects, and learning how things work. That curiosity slowly grew into a serious interest in programming, software development, and building things that can actually be useful.

I enjoy working across different areas of technology, including web development, object-oriented programming, technical support, and user-focused design.

What excites me most is the process of learning something new, applying it, and watching an idea turn into something real. Projects like my full-stack application, 8 Queens visual game, and Commvault prototype helped me practice persistence, problem-solving, teamwork, and creativity.

Beyond technology, I love art, design, drawing, music, singing, and playing instruments. I believe creativity helps me approach technical problems in a more thoughtful and human-centered way.

My goal is to grow as a software engineer and build solutions that are not only functional, but also useful, accessible, and meaningful for the people who use them.
